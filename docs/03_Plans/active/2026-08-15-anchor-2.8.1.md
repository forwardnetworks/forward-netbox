# Advance the release provenance anchor to 2.8.1

## Goal

Move `PRIOR_RELEASE_TAG` and `PRIOR_POST_RELEASE_DOC_COMMIT` onto `v2.8.1` and
its bridge, and promote 2.8.1 to current in the compatibility tables.

## Why

The verifier walks the first-parent lineage from the anchor bridge to the tag
being published. Left on `v2.8.0` that range grows with every commit, and a
range large enough for GitHub to expire the run burns a release at the tag -
which is what happened to `v2.6.10`.

The table promotion belongs in the same change: `check_harness.py` fails while
the anchor disagrees with whichever release the table calls current.

## Constraints

- The bridge must already be on `main` and be the first first-parent commit
  after the tag. `5547078` is, via #213.
- `v2.8.1` published, so it is NOT recorded in `UNPUBLISHED_RELEASE_TAGS`.
- Five sites carry the anchor, two of them outside
  `verify_release_provenance.py`.

## Touched Surfaces

- `scripts/verify_release_provenance.py` - both anchor constants
- `scripts/check_harness.py`, `.github/workflows/release.yml`,
  `scripts/tests/test_tasks.py`
- `README.md`, `docs/README.md`, `docs/01_User_Guide/README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`

## Approach

Mechanical. The bridge SHA is read from
`git rev-list --first-parent v2.8.1..origin/main | tail -1` rather than copied,
and the promotion goes through `promote_release_candidate_text` and
`gen_changelog.py` so the three tables and the changelog cannot drift apart.

## Validation

`scripts/check_harness.py`, which enforces both the anchor/table agreement and
the `release.yml` shape, and `scripts/tests`.

## Rollback

Revert. The anchor returns to `v2.8.0`, stale but not broken until the reviewed
range grows large enough to expire a run.

## Decision Log

- **Promote the table in the same commit.** The harness couples them, so
  splitting them guarantees one red commit.
- **Do not list `v2.8.1` in `UNPUBLISHED_RELEASE_TAGS`.** It published: PyPI has
  the wheel and sdist with provenance attestations, and the GitHub release
  carries both plus the SBOM.

## Open

- Nothing for 2.8.1. #206 stays open for the adapter-only drift models.
