# Advance the release provenance anchor to 2.9.0

## Goal

Move the provenance anchor onto the shipped 2.9.0 release and promote it to
current in the compatibility tables.

## Why

The anchor names the previous release and the documentation-only bridge commit
that must directly follow its tag; until it advances, `check_harness.py` fails
by construction, which is the check working.

v2.9.0 published: the tag peels to `d93011f9637f9cce87e8c4ab52eb708276e53463`,
the release workflow succeeded, and PyPI (`forward-netbox` 2.9.0) and GitHub
(`v2.9.0`, not draft, not prerelease) both carry the release.

## Constraints

- The bridge must be the first commit after the tag on the first-parent lineage
  and documentation-only. `5c9ff8f` is one file, no code.
- The table promotion belongs here, not on the release branch.

## Touched Surfaces

- `scripts/verify_release_provenance.py`, `scripts/check_harness.py`,
  `scripts/tests/test_tasks.py`, `.github/workflows/release.yml`
- `README.md`, `docs/README.md`, `docs/01_User_Guide/README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`

## Approach

Anchor to `v2.9.0` / bridge `5c9ff8f83e0eb082a60aae50b32e9c97cdac5f9a`; promote
2.9.0 to current, demote 2.8.9 to superseded.

## Validation

`check_harness.py` passes with the anchor advanced; `scripts/tests` passes;
`verify_release_provenance.py --controls-only` passes against live GitHub.

## Rollback

Revert; the harness fails again, which blocks the next release rather than
permitting an unverified one - the safe direction.

## Decision Log

- Promotion folded into the anchor commit, as for 2.8.6-2.8.9.

## Open

- Whether unmanaged-but-collected devices should get a separate config-backup
  prefix remains an unanswered product question, carried from the 2.9.0 plan.
- GitHub's Dependabot flagged 4 vulnerabilities (3 high, 1 moderate) on the
  default branch after this tag pushed; not yet triaged against
  `ACCEPTED_DEPENDENCY_ADVISORIES` or `constraints.txt` - separate follow-up,
  not part of this anchor.
